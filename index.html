    What this is
    A demonstration of how SGR helps people govern changes to building information. The knowledge graph inside this page stores the building information; the Review tab makes it understandable and lets an operator review each proposed correction before anything changes.
    
    
    What is real and what is simulated
    The graph and the rule checks are real: the rules run on the stored triples every time, and approved corrections are written back with a decision record. The proposed corrections, the evidence and the work orders are scripted. No language model, SHACL engine or database runs here, and nothing is sent anywhere.
    
    
    Where the examples come from
    AHU-02, VAV-03, Mechanical Room 01 and Room R6-12 are example data. FCU-6F-10, CH-1, CH-2 and room R6-08 follow the documented 6F baseline graph, including the control drawing that shows one analog input for return air temperature.
