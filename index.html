    What this is

    A demonstration of how SGR helps people govern changes to building information. The knowledge graph stores the building information; this page makes it understandable and lets an operator review each proposed correction before anything changes.

    What is simulated

    The rule checks, the proposed corrections, the validation and the work orders are scripted for this demo. No language model, SHACL engine or database runs here, and nothing is sent anywhere. Your decisions stay in this browser tab.

    Where the examples come from

    AHU-02, VAV-03, Mechanical Room 01 and Room R6-12 are example data. FCU-6F-10, CH-1 and room R6-08 follow the documented 6F baseline graph, including the control drawing that shows one analog input for return air temperature.
